\begin{abstract}
We present a closed-loop computational pipeline for superconductor candidate discovery designed for unified-memory consumer workstations (AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395 / Strix Halo APU with Radeon 8060S GPU and 128 GB unified LPDDR5X). The architecture couples four stages: (1) compositional Magpie feature screening trained on 21,263 entries of the UCI SuperCon dataset ($\mathrm{MAE} = 5.36\text{ K}$, $R^2 = 0.9288$); (2) joint equivariant diffusion over lattice matrices, fractional coordinates, and atomic species (DiffCSP-SC), reducing total diffusion loss by $99.01\%$ ($152.00 \to 1.50$) over 1,000 epochs; (3) native C++/HIP zero-copy pre-relaxation (\texttt{hipHostMallocMapped} and \texttt{torch::from\_blob}) with a $1.20\text{ \AA}$ hard-core collision filter that eliminates PCIe host-device transfer overhead ($144.7\text{ ms/step}$); and (4) first-principles plane-wave Density Functional Theory (DFT) verification using Quantum ESPRESSO v7.3 (PBE-GGA, PAW pseudopotentials, $50\text{ Ry} / 400\text{ Ry}$ cutoffs). Across 50 diffusion-generated candidates, the top five chemically distinct structures ($\mathrm{CeIn}_3$, $\mathrm{Sb}_2\mathrm{Te}_3$, $\mathrm{BaBiTe}$, $\mathrm{AuPd}_{11}$, and $\mathrm{CuIn}_2\mathrm{K}$) achieved $100\%$ SCF convergence and $100\%$ metallic character. In particular, $\mathrm{BaBiTe}$ emerges as a pressure-stabilized ($\sim 18.9\text{ GPa}$) metallic ternary candidate ($E_{\mathrm{tot}} = -1704.43\text{ Ry}$, $E_F = 2.98\text{ eV}$, residual forces $0.021\text{ Ry/Bohr}$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Pipeline Architecture}
On unified-memory APUs (AMD Strix Halo GFX1151), allocating atomic coordinate and force buffers via \texttt{hipHostMalloc(..., hipHostMallocMapped)} and wrapping them with \texttt{torch::from\_blob} provides identical host and device virtual pointers (\texttt{cpu\_ptr == gpu\_ptr}), achieving $144.7\text{ ms/step}$ during TorchScript MACE pre-relaxation with zero PCIe copy overhead. A pre-MACE minimum-image collision filter enforces $d_{ij} \ge 1.20\text{ \AA}$ to prevent gradient divergence on raw diffusion samples.

\section{Limitations}
While static SCF calculations confirm electronic stability, small residual forces ($0.021\text{ Ry/Bohr}$ for $\mathrm{BaBiTe}$), and metallic Fermi surfaces, full DFPT phonon dispersion $\omega(\mathbf{q})$, Eliashberg $\alpha^2 F(\omega)$ integration, and ternary convex-hull enthalpy comparisons remain necessary prior to experimental synthesis.
