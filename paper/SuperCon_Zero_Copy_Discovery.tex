These are static calculations on unrelaxed generated cells. They establish SCF convergence only; no relaxation, convex-hull, completed phonon or electron--phonon calculation is reported, and no $T_c$ is claimed. The +189\,kbar residual stress of BaBiTe indicates a cell far from equilibrium. On Strix Halo there is no PCIe link between CPU and iGPU; the benefit of the zero-copy design must be shown by a same-GPU mapped-vs-copied benchmark, which is pending.
